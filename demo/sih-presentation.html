Innovation & Uniqueness

🧠 Vision-First Redaction

Face detection reads the raw frame first — catching faces and PII that DOM parsing cannot see.

💻 Offline On-Device ML

4 self-hosted models (ViT, BlazeFace, Tesseract, DistilBERT) run fully in-browser, offline-capable.
